Workout Program (4-Day Split)

Day 1: Chest + Triceps

  • Bench Press 4x6-10
  • Incline Dumbbell Press 3x8-12
  • Chest Dips 3x10-12
  • Cable Crossover 3x12-15
  • Overhead Triceps Extension 3x10-12
  • Rope Pushdown 3x12-15
  • Skull Crushers 3x10

Day 2: Back + Biceps

  • Back Extension 4x5-8
  • Pull-Ups / Lat Pulldown 4x8-12
  • Barbell Row 3x8-10
  • Seated Cable Row 3x10-12
  • Dumbbell Curl 3x10-12
  • Hammer Curl 3x12-15
  • Concentration Curl 3x12-15

Day 3: Legs + Calves

  • Squat 4x6-10
  • Bulgarian Split Squat 3x10/leg
  • Leg Press 3x10-12
  • Leg Extension 3x12-15
  • Standing Calf Raise 4x15-20
  • Seated Calf Raise 3x20

Day 4: Shoulders + Core

  • Overhead Press 4x6-10
  • Arnold Press 3x8-10
  • Lateral Raise 4x12-15
  • Front Raise 3x10-12
  • Rear Delt Fly 3x10-12
  • Face Pull 3x10-12
