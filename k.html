Notes & Tips

  • Drink 2-3 liters of water daily.
  • Limit salt, sugar, and fried foods.
  • Allow one small cheat meal every 7-10 days in moderation.
  • Aim for 7-8 hours of sleep per night.
  • Track progress weekly: weight, measurements, photos.
  • Reduce stress to aid fat loss.
