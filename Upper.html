Upper/Lower Split Workout Program

Day 1 - Upper (Chest + Back + Shoulders + Biceps + Triceps)

  • Barbell Bench Press 4x6-8
  • Incline Dumbbell Press 3x8-10
  • Chest Fly / Cable Fly 3x12-15
  • Pull-Ups / Lat Pulldown 4x6-10
  • Barbell or Dumbbell Row 3x8-10
  • Face Pulls / Rear Delt Fly 3x12-15
  • Overhead Dumbbell Press 3x8-10
  • Biceps Curl 3x10-12
  • Triceps Pushdown / Overhead Extension 3x10-12

Day 2 - Lower (Legs + Abs)

  • Back Squat / Front Squat 4x6-8
  • Romanian Deadlift 3x8-10
  • Walking Lunges / Bulgarian Split Squat 3x12 each leg
  • Leg Press / Leg Extension 3x10-12
  • Hamstring Curl 3x12-15
  • Standing Calf Raises 4x12-15
  • Plank + Hanging Leg Raise 3x30-60 sec / 12-15

Day 3 - Rest or Light Activity

  • Stretching, walking, or cardio (20-30 minutes)

Day 4 - Upper (Variation of Day 1)

  • Incline Barbell Press 4x6-8
  • Flat Dumbbell Press 3x8-10
  • Cable Crossover 3x12-15
  • Pull-Ups (Neutral Grip) 4x6-10
  • Seated Cable Row 3x8-10
  • Dumbbell Lateral Raise 3x12-15
  • Face Pull / Rear Delt Machine 3x12-15
  • Incline Dumbbell Curl 3x10-12
  • Skull Crushers / Overhead Extension 3x10-12

Day 5 - Lower (Variation of Day 2)

  • Deadlift / Trap Bar Deadlift 4x5-6
  • Walking Lunges / Step-Ups 3x12 each leg
  • Leg Press (different angle) 3x10-12
  • Seated Leg Curl 3x12-15
  • Standing Calf Raises 4x12-15
  • Cable Woodchopper / Russian Twist 3x12-15 each side
